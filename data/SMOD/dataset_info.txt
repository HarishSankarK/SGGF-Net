SMOD Dataset - Preprocessed
==================================================

Original Classes (COCO format):
  1: person
  2: rider
  3: bicycle
  4: car

YOLO Format Classes:
  0: person
  1: rider
  2: bicycle
  3: car

2-Class System (after remapping in dataset loader):
  0: background
  1: person (from classes 0 and 1)
  2: vehicle (from classes 2 and 3)

Splits:
  train: 4800 images
  val: 600 images
  test: 3276 images
